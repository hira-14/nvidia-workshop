Application landscape

Variant effect prediction

Supervised Clinical

Predict pathogenicity of SNVs/indels: AlphaMissense, PrimateAI-3D, EVE. Input: sequence + conservation + structure. Output: probability pathogenic.

Regulatory genomics

Supervised Sequence → function

Predict chromatin accessibility, TF binding, histone marks, enhancer activity from DNA sequence: Enformer, BPNet, Sei. Input: 200k bp → 5k tracks.

Gene expression & splicing

Supervised Multi-omics

RNA-seq quantification (Salmon, kallisto), splicing prediction (SpliceAI), single-cell annotation (scVI, scBERT, GeneFormer).

Protein structure & design

Self-supervised Structure

AlphaFold2/3, ESMFold, RoseTTAFold. MSA + pair representation → 3D coordinates. Inverse folding (ProteinMPNN) for design.

Genome assembly & polishing

Self-supervised Long reads

Basecalling (Bonito, Dorado), assembly (hifiasm, Flye), polishing (DeepPolisher), scaffolding (YaHS). Raw signal → consensus.

Population & evolutionary genomics

Unsupervised Population

Demographic inference (fastsimcoal, MSMC), selection scans (SweepFinder, diploSHIC), ancestry (PCA, ADMIXTURE, neural net versions).

Deep dive: Variant effect prediction

Clinical genetics' hardest problem: given a variant (e.g., BRCA1 c.68_69delAG), is it pathogenic? Three paradigm shifts:

EraApproachLimitation
2010–2015Conservation scores (PhyloP, GERP), SIFT, PolyPhen-2Heuristic; miss gain-of-function; poor on non-coding
2016–2021Supervised on ClinVar: CNNs/Transformers (PrimAI, CADD)Labels noisy; circularity (ClinVar uses same predictors); limited non-coding
2022+Unsupervised / evolutionary: EVE, AlphaMissense, ESM1vNo labels needed; learns from MSA / protein LM; generalizes to unseen variants

Key insight: Protein language models (ESM, ProGen) trained on millions of sequences learn evolutionary constraints implicitly. Masked prediction likelihood = fitness proxy. AlphaMissense adds structural context from AlphaFold.

Deep dive: Regulatory prediction (Enformer architecture)

Predict 5,313 epigenetic tracks (CAGE, ATAC, ChIP-seq, RNA-seq) from 196,608 bp DNA sequence.

Input: 196,608 bp → one-hot [196608, 4] ↓ Stem: 7× ConvBlock (pool 2×) → [1536, 1536] ↓ 11× Transformer (8 heads, dim 1536) → [1536, 1536] ↓ Crop center 896 bp → [896, 1536] ↓ 2× ConvBlock (dilated) → [896, 1536] ↓ Task heads: 5,313 outputs (profile + total)

Why it works: CNN stem captures local motifs; Transformer integrates distal enhancers (up to 100k bp away); multi-task across tracks shares representations. Limitation: Human reference only; no personal haplotype input.

Deep dive: Single-cell foundation models

scRNA-seq: 10k–1M cells × 20k genes. Sparse, noisy, batch effects. Foundation models:

  • scVI / scANVI (2018–2021): VAE for batch correction + label transfer. Generative, not transformer.
  • GeneFormer (2023): Transformer on rank-normalized gene expression; pre-train on 30M cells → fine-tune for disease classification, perturbation prediction.
  • scGPT (2023): BERT-style masking on tokenized gene expression + gene embeddings; pre-train 10M cells.
  • Universal Cell Embedding (UCE, 2024): Contrastive learning across 36M cells; zero-shot cell type annotation.

Emerging pattern: Pre-train on massive public atlases (Tabula Sapiens, Human Cell Atlas) → transfer to small disease cohorts.
